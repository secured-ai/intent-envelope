# Disclosure under a deadline

The Chainalysis briefing: a first disclosure said non-sensitive identity metadata, and
five days later a second said password hashes and live tokens for every user. A first
statement is a sworn statement you will be measured against for a year.

**Owner:** [name]. **Who signs the statement:** [name].

## The rule

Keep two lists from the first hour, and never move anything from the second to the first
without evidence:

- **Established.** What the records prove.
- **Not yet established.** Everything else, with the reason it is not yet known.

The ledger's disclosure export is built this way, and its second half is a draft of
your caveats: a broken chain, a window the ledger does not cover, unsigned entries,
targets only the agent described, actions that went around the boundary, and the
standing fact that an allowed call is a permission, not proof the tool completed.

## Writing the first statement

1. **Say what is established, in the records' terms.** "Between [time] and [time], the
   agent [identity] was permitted to [action] on [target]."
2. **Say what is not yet established, and that it is being established.** Not "there is
   no evidence of", which reads as "it did not happen" and is the sentence the Commission
   briefing turned on.
3. **Do not round down.** If coverage was not measured, the established list is a
   floor, and the statement must not read as a total.
4. **Date every later update against the first,** and say what moved from the second
   list to the first and on what evidence.
